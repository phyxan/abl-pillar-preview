Their first account, and the habit that outlasts it

The Allied Rising Star Account is a savings account for children under 18, opened and operated with a parent or guardian. It starts at Rs 100. The savings sit in a Main Account, and the daily profit lands in a separate Wallet Account the child can watch grow.

Why two accounts

The child sees two numbers, and learns the difference

Most children's accounts hide the thing worth teaching. Here the money saved and the money the savings earned sit in different places, so the lesson needs no explaining.

The money they put in

Every deposit goes to the Main Account. A child who counts the notes at the counter remembers the balance better than one who is told it.

The money it earns

Profit is worked out and credited daily to the Wallet Account. Watching a second number appear on its own is the clearest lesson in saving a child can get.

The habit it builds

Pocket money paid on the same day each week, a fixed share saved before anything is spent, and a monthly look at the balance together.

Parents ask us

Questions, answered plainly

Who can open an Allied Rising Star Account?

The account is for children and minors under 18. It belongs to the child, and a parent or guardian opens and operates it. On the Shariah-compliant version the guardian may be a parent, the natural guardian, who is the father, or a court-appointed guardian for a minor up to 21.

How much do I need to open it?

Rs 100 opens the account.

Is there a minimum balance to maintain?

No. The account carries no minimum balance requirement.

What documents do I need to bring?

The child's B-Form or Juvenile Card, your CNIC, SNIC or NICOP, and your proof of income, such as a salary slip or a business letterhead. The branch may ask for anything else required under bank policy.

What is a B-Form, and why does the bank need it?

The B-Form, formally the Child Registration Certificate, is issued by NADRA and records a child's identity before they are old enough for a CNIC. The bank needs it to open an account in the child's name. Most parents arrange it first, and it is the step that usually takes the longest.

Where does the profit go?

Profit is calculated and credited daily to the Wallet Account, while the Main Account holds the savings. Profit rates change, so this page does not print one. The current figures are on the projected profit rates page.

How much can be withdrawn at one time?

A single debit transaction is capped at PKR 100,000.

Can my child use the debit card on their own?

Account activities, including card use, are carried out with parental assistance, so banking stays supervised. The card itself is usable globally with parental access.

What does the account come with?

A debit card, two cheque books, one for each account, internet banking and SMS alerts. The child also receives a welcome gift, a first account certificate, a cash box, a personalised ID card and a birthday gift every year. Free insurance cover in case of mishap is included.

Can I open the account online?

The account is opened at a branch, with the documents listed above.

Is there a Shariah-compliant version?

Yes. The Allied Aitebar Rising Star Account from Allied Islamic Aitebar Banking works on a Mudarabah basis, with a guardian saving account and a pocket money current account for the minor, and free accidental takaful cover up to Rs 500,000 for the guardian.

What happens when my child turns 18?

The banking relationship continues with the Allied Youth Account, which is for customers aged 18 to 35.

Start them at Rs 100

Bring the child's B-Form, your identity document and proof of income to any Allied Bank branch. The account, and the cash box, go home the same day.
